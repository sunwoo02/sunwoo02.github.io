I am an undergraduate researcher at KAIST, currently intern at the [COCO Lab](https://coco-kaist.github.io/) under the supervision of
[Hyunwoo Kim](https://hyunw.kim/). My research centers on the foundations of reasoning in large language models. I am interested in how
reinforcement learning and post-training methods shape reasoning behavior, and in developing principled approaches that
improve robustness, generalization, and socially grounded inference.
